ABOUT US

TrueCore Traders is a leading supplier of industrial materials, tools, and equipment for major projects across Saudi Arabia. We cater to a wide range of sectors including construction, oil & gas, infrastructure, and facility maintenance.

With a focus on quality, safety, and timely delivery, we provide everything from safety gear and electrical items to scaffolding systems and mechanical supplies. Our goal is to support our clients with reliable products and responsive service—helping them build safely, efficiently, and on schedule.

OUR VISION

To be the leading and most trusted supplier of industrial and construction materials across Saudi Arabia—powering progress through quality, safety, and reliability.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to provide high-quality tools, equipment, and materials that meet the evolving needs of industrial and infrastructure projects in the Kingdom. We are committed to timely delivery, customer satisfaction, and building long-term partnerships based on trust and performance.

Supplying Saudi Arabia's growth with quality industrial materials since day one.
