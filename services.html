OUR SERVICES & PRODUCTS

At TrueCore Traders, we supply a comprehensive range of industrial and commercial materials to meet the demands of Saudi Arabia's growing projects. Our offerings include:

01

Safety Equipment & Gear

We supply personal protective equipment and workplace safety gear for construction and industrial sites across Saudi Arabia, including safety helmets, high-visibility vests, safety glasses, cut-resistant gloves, steel-toe safety boots, fall protection harnesses, and safety signage.

02

Power & Hand Tools

Our hand tools are built for daily use on construction and industrial sites, including adjustable spanners, combination wrench sets, screwdriver sets, utility knives, measuring tapes, pliers, and hammers.

03

Electrical Items

We supply electrical materials and components for new installations and maintenance work, including cables, switches, circuit breakers, conduit fittings, and junction boxes.

04

Civil & Mechanical Materials

We provide a broad range of civil and mechanical construction materials, supporting project needs from groundwork through structural and mechanical installation across contractor and engineering projects.

05

Lifting Equipment

Our lifting gear supports safe material handling on construction and industrial sites, including slings, shackles, chain blocks, wire rope, and rigging accessories.

06

Testing Instruments

We supply test and measurement instruments for quality control, inspection, and compliance checks on industrial and construction projects, including multimeters, pressure gauges, and calibration tools.

07

Welding Tools & Supplies

Our welding and construction tools support fabrication and site work, including auto-darkening welding helmets, leather welding gloves, welding torch sets, paint rollers, plaster scrapers, and heavy-duty hand shovels.

08

Fasteners & Hardware

We stock a full range of fasteners and hardware for construction and industrial applications, including bolts, nuts, screws, washers, and anchors, in stock for fast project turnaround.

09

Lighting Fixtures

We supply industrial and commercial lighting fixtures suited to construction sites, warehouses, and facility installations, including LED floodlights, industrial high-bay lights, and site lighting.

10

Furniture & Plumbing Materials

Our furniture and plumbing supplies support commercial and industrial facility fit-outs and renovations, including pipes, fittings, valves, and site furniture.

11

Cleaning & Maintenance Supplies

We provide cleaning and janitorial supplies for industrial sites, warehouses, and commercial premises, including mop buckets with wringers, microfiber cleaning cloths, floor squeegees, disinfectant solutions, cleaning trolleys, and heavy-duty garbage bags.

12

Office Supplies

We supply office stationery and supplies for site offices and administrative operations, including A4 paper reams, staplers, file folders, desk organizers, notepads, and scissors & cutters.

13

Scaffolding & Access Systems

Our scaffolding and access equipment supports safe working at height on construction and industrial sites, including scaffold frames, planks, ladders, and guardrails.
